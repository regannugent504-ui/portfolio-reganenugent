About Me

I was born and raised in Metairie, Louisiana, just 20 minutes away from New Orleans, one of the biggest cities in the world for art and creative expression. I grew up doing musical theater, performing in a variety of productions across Louisiana, which helped spark my love for storytelling and working with other creative visionaries. All throughout my life, creativity has played a central role in who I am and what I am passionate about.

I am currently a third-year undergraduate student at Texas Christian University's Bob Schieffer College of Communications, double-majoring in Strategic Communication and Digital Culture and Data Analytics. I thoroughly enjoy turning ideas into content that not only connects with the right audience but also strengthens brand identity through strategy, creativity, and intent.

In Alpha Delta Pi, I serve as the Director of Public Relations, where I issue news releases, assist in strategic social media planning, publicize and market all chapter information for members, and provide updates about sisters to the chapter on a weekly basis. Apart from Greek life, I also serve as a dedicated member of various clubs and organizations, including the Public Relations Student Society of America, TheEnd, and Letters of Love TCU.

Outside of campus involvement, I have gained hands-on experience in public relations from my various campus ambassador positions. Through my work for Olive and June, Bubble Skincare, IPSY, and Flipd, I have developed skills such as content strategy, data analysis, and audience insights. From TikTok videos to Instagram posts, I have learned how to not only reel in an audience but also how to keep them engaged.

I adore environments that involve collaboration, and I am especially drawn to opportunities that promote creativity, research and analysis, and communication. At the end of the day, no matter what task I am put to, I can guarantee that my ambition and hard work will always shine through.
